Comments:
This paper proposes a reversible data hiding method in the encrypted, which improves the embedding capacity. Overall, the authors' proposed scheme indeed outperforms the SOTA schemes. However, I am afraid that the main problem with this paper is the lack of a detailed explanation of the innovations. The problem with the current manuscript is that the proposed scheme is almost identical to the key technologies used in existing SOTA schemes [20], while at the same time, the authors do not explain their innovativeness sufficiently. Besides, the experimental part should be modified and supplemented as necessary. Therefore, the current version of the manuscript is not publishable in IEEE T-MM until the authors make a more intuitive statement about the innovativeness of the article. The author cannot expect the reader to seek out your innovations from the description of your methods.
The specific problems are as follows:
1.      The key techniques used in the current manuscript and reference [20] are identical, which is reflected in the fact that their titles overlap almost completely. Therefore the authors should have highlighted their contribution in the Abstract and Introduction sections by making more detailed explanations. Unfortunately, under the current revision, the authors still present their contribution in very general terms. The authors should appropriately revise and contextualize their response to Reviewer 2, " And during our journey of composing this article, we by chance discovered the other two interesting improvements, especially the improvement about REF bytes, which we have not seen in any of our reference papers. Since this improvement can be adopted in almost any error-prediction based papers, we believe it is worth mentioning to share this idea to other researchers.", and add it to their contributions to the abstract and introduction. If possible, it is recommended that the authors write a separate section detailing the motivation and related contributions.
2.      In the experimental section, it is inappropriate for the authors to divide the subsections under the title of datasets. Each performance of the algorithm, such as security, runtime, capacity, etc., should be analyzed and discussed separately. The division of experimental sections can be referred to reference [20]. In addition to the BOSSbase and BOWS-2 datasets, it is recommended to supplement the experiments on the UCID dataset.
3.      The English usage in the paper should be reviewed carefully, and the writing should be improved.
[20] Z. Yin, Y. Xiang and X. Zhang, "Reversible Data Hiding in Encrypted Images Based on Multi-MSB Prediction and Huffman Coding," in IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, vol. 22, no. 4, pp. 874-884, April 2020, doi: 10.1109/TMM.2019.2936314.
